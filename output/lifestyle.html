Paris Hilton Is a ‘Slave 4 U’ in 4th Annual Britney Spears Halloween Costume: ‘Such a Legendary Moment in Pop Music’ (Exclusive Photos)

📅 Oct 23, 2026  |  Category: Lifestyle  |  Read Source →

What is Paris Hilton‘s costume for Halloween 2026? It’s Britney, b—h (again)!

In photos exclusively shared with Billboard on Friday (Oct. 23), the mogul debuts her spooky-season tribute to longtime friend and pop icon Britney Spears, specifically channeling her performance of “I’m a Slave 4 U” at the MTV Video Music Awards in 2001. In the snaps by Brian Ziff, Hilton models a re-creation of the Princess of Pop’s green bandeau and bejeweled shorts — and yes, she even topped it off with an albino Burmese python resembling the one Spears famously carried on her shoulders on the awards show stage 25 years ago.

“Britney is one of the most iconic artists of our generation, and ‘I’m a Slave 4 U’ will always be such a legendary moment in pop music,” Hilton tells Billboard exclusively. “The song, the choreography, the fashion — it was everything. I’ve loved re-creating her different looks over the years because each one represents such a special moment in music history.”

She added: “Britney is not only an icon, but also a dear friend, and I’ll always love celebrating her and the incredible impact she’s had on all of us.”

This isn’t the first year Hilton has transformed into Spears for Halloween. In 2025, the DJ sported a shiny red jumpsuit and a teased ponytail to emulate the “Oops!… I Did It Again” music video, and the year before that, she re-created Spears’ schoolgirl look in the “… Baby One More Time” visual. In 2023, she celebrated Hallow’s Eve in a blue flight attendant getup à la Brit in the 2003 “Toxic” music video.

At this point, it’s clear that Hilton is one of Spears’ biggest fans — and the love goes both ways. The singer wrote in her The Woman in Me memoir that the Simple Life alum was “one of the people who was kindest to me when I really needed kindness.”

“I was crying when I read that,” Hilton later said of the passage on The Kelly Clarkson Show. “That really touched me. She’s just so strong. I love her so much, and I’m just so proud of her.”

North West Dresses as Natalie Nunn for Halloween: Here’s How the ‘Bad Girls Club’ Star Reacted

📅 Oct 22, 2026  |  Category: Lifestyle  |  Read Source →

North West could have gone the obvious route and returned the favor to her mom after Kim Kardashian dressed up as her eldest daughter for an early Halloween gag. But instead, the aspiring 13-year-old rapper paid tribute to a different reality TV icon on Wednesday (Oct. 21) when she unveiled her 2026 dress-up choice.

In a TikTok that has already rolled up more than one million likes and nearly 8 million views cued to a Nicki Minaj lyric from her 2023 collab on Lil Uzi Vert’s “Endless Fashion” — “Even if my name was Natalie Nunn/ None of these b–ches still couldn’t chin check me” — North lip syncs along and then transforms from her blue-haired self into Baddies star Natalie Nunn.

And, keeping it 100, the costume includes a wildly exaggerated version of Nunn’s notoriously prominent chin, as well as a replica of Nunn’s glittering “CEO Baddies” chain. North West posted a series of TikToks featuring her lip syncing to Nunn quotes, showing off her “Baddies” bling and doing an A-B comparison between her all-black outfit and some of Nunn’s looks. And, wouldn’t you know it? A short time later, Bad Girls Club alum Nunn weighed in on the homage.

“I GOTTA GET @northwest her baddie chain now! ❤️❤️❤️❤️ SHE GETS A BADDIE CHAIN HAPPY HALLOWEEN MONTH !!” she wrote on Instagram in a repost of North’s original video.

North’s bid to be the crowned the early Halloween Queen came just a day after mom Kim mimicked her daughter’s emo look in an Instagram photo shoot on Tuesday (Oct. 20) in which she rocked a North4evr shirt — a reference to the title of North’s 2026 EP — along with a turquoise wig, hand and face piercings, an iced-out chain, spiky metal bracelets and metallic nail art.

The comments were filled with praise from fans, as well as Kim’s mom, Kris Jenner, who wrote “This is so fun,” and sister Khloe, who said, “Twins!!!!”

Though they’ve so far gone their own ways this year, in the past mother and daughter have teamed up for joint costumes, including in 2023 when they dressed up as Cher and Dionne from Clueless and in 2019, when the Kardashian-West family channeled The Flintstones; Kardashian’s divorce from ex-husband Ye, North’s father, was finalized in 2022.

‘Kpop Demon Hunters’ Singer EJAE Marries Music Producer Samuel Kim in Seoul

📅 Oct 20, 2026  |  Category: Lifestyle  |  Read Source →

KPop Demon Hunters “Golden” vocalist EJAE married her longtime fiancé music producer Samuel Kim on Saturday (Oct. 17) in Seoul, South Korea. According to Vogue, the ceremony was infused with nods to the couple’s heritage and kicked off with a Paebaek ceremony, a centuries-old Korean tradition that originally served as a welcome for the bride into the groom’s family.

The ceremony and welcome party took place at the Korean fusion restaurant Congdu50, which has sweeping views of the capital city. “Not everyone does Paebaek, but we wanted to have that traditional element as part of our wedding,” EJAE told the magazine.

The couple then exchanged vows on Saturday at Stage 28, a venue whose origin story had deep meaning for EJAE, as her grandparents lived in the house on the venue property when she was a girl. “I spent much of my childhood exploring the garden and walking with my grandfather in the hills behind the house,” she explained of the home and property that were later turned into an event venue, where EJAE’s brother also got married.

The singer reposted a gallery of photos from the event on Instagram on Monday (Oct. 19), opening with a snap of the happy couple raising their arms in joy as they walk down the aisle after the ceremony. The bride wore a custom Daniele Frankel white tulle gown with a strapless neckline, a bubbled hem and a nude chiffon layer that Vogue said was in keeping with the singer’s desire for a dress that would be “ethereal, modern, chic and timeless with a twist.” She accented the gown with heirloom jewelry passed down from her grandmother to her mother.

For the party, EJAE changed into a custom Zac Posen chiffon dress and, later in the night, a shimmery Milla Nova mini dress for dancing. The groom wore a bespoke double-breasted black tuxedo from Bhambi’s Custom Tailors in New York, as well as a custom white tuxedo by Posen for the reception. The couple wore traditional Korean ceremonial garments for their Paebaek ceremony.

Their love story began in 2017 when they were set up by a mutual friend for a songwriting session at Kim’s home studio. “We worked on a couple of songs, but mostly, we just connected,” Ejae told the fashion magazine about the relationship that was strictly professional until the night she went out to see Kim’s roommate’s band perform. “We both loved anime and the same kind of music … Later that night, [he] confessed his feelings. We’ve been together ever since.”

They got a place together in Brooklyn and then, after more than seven years, Kim (tried) to surprise the singer by taking her for a walk through their favorite borough park last June, where their friends had set up a picnic and he popped the question. Shortly after, KPop Demon Hunters was released and suddenly EJAE’s work as part of the fictional K-pop group HUNTR/X on the Billboard Hot 100 No. 1 hit “Golden” shot her into the pop stratosphere.

The endless promotional and awards show cycle meant that wedding plans had to be put on hold for a while after EJAE said they initially planned a November 2026 wedding in California, before moving it up to this month in South Korea. “That left us about four months to plan everything,” she said. “It was hectic and a little stressful, but it was real teamwork, and everything has fallen into place perfectly.”

EJEA told Vogue that the ceremony was “beautiful, heartfelt and fun … I’ll never forget the feeling of walking down the aisle and seeing all of my closest friends and family members surrounding me with their love and support. Sam and I had set intentions to try to stay in the moment and connect with each other and our guests. We definitely were both a bit nervous, but mostly excited to start our lives together as husband and wife.”
