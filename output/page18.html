Probably Stolen Week 1 walkthrough

The first week is where you begin your trade and set up your shop. First, pick a Specialization from the complete list of options available. Easiest one is Apprentice if you want things to begin smoothly.

Screenshot by Destructoid

Next, pick the starting Perks that you’ll want to shape your save. Similarly, you can pick from between three different difficulty modifier options. The game will give you a quick tutorial of the overlay, and you’re all set then for your first piece of business.

Day 1

There will be five customers to attend, and you can call them using the green button near the recorder. At first, you’ll find your mentor who guides you about the basics. The customers who appear will be randomized, so the ones I encountered will be different from the ones you do.

Ideally, I looked to buy all types of food items since they can be sold for a profit. You’ll also be provided with a list of criminals; if one of them appears, make sure to inform the authorities. This specific encounter is fixed for all players.

Day 2

Screenshot by Destructoid

Again the same pattern follows, and you will have four customers to attend to. Once again, I looked to purchase any food item they had, or discounted stuff they looked to sell for cheap. Do note that not every discounted item will be profitable, but you ultimately go green eventually.

After the second day, you’ll be able to visit the Dumping Ground. Once you exhaust all attempts, pick the most valuable item and return.

Day 3

On the third day, you’ll have six customers to attend. However, one of them will be your mentor, and you’ll learn about how to check discrepancies. You can use your magnifying glass to inspect closely whether the product you’re about to buy has any defects or not.

Day 4

On the fourth day, you’ll come across the labeler. The labeler allows you to fix counterfeit items that raise their values. I also came across one of the six criminals notified on day, and decided to call the authority.

Day 5

Screenshot by Destructoid

On the fifth day, you’ll get to establish your display case. You can keep an assorted list of items in this case. Do note that the types of items will keep will attract different kind of customers to your shop. On my run, a second bounty target appeared, and I once again decided to call the authority on them.

Day 6

The sixth day didn’t have anything worthwhile happening on my run. By now, you’ll find some really valuable items to purchase from few customers. If you have the credits, don’t hesitate to buy.

Day 7

The final day of the first week has the highest number of clients to meet. Moreover, you’ll get to encounter someone named Wild, and you’ll learn about upgrades. Known as Wild’s Favours, you can pick from a wide variety of upgrades.

Your picks will largely depend on how your run is proceeding, and what kind of items you need.

Stay tuned for the walkthrough for the second week and all its days.

How to play Globle

To play Globle, you’re first going to have to access the game’s website. To start playing, simply enter in the name of any country of your choice. You’ll then get a colour match for that country indicating how close to or far away you are from the answer.

The deeper the colour, the closer you are. After a couple of guesses you will slowly be able to narrow down the answer. A good tip is to pick larger countries that share borders with other countries. You can clear large sections of the map this way. Early on, you might also want to try varying your guesses between countries in different continents to narrow down your options.

The last couple of years have seen a huge rise in browser-based puzzle games, tasking players with working out a certain kind of answer using limited guesses. Framed is one of the newest, following in the footsteps of Wordle, but offering a slightly different twist. You’ll still need to work out the answer using limited information and only six tries, but it’ll be movies that you’ll be guessing.

  • READ MORE: ‘Connections’ answers and hints for today

You see, Framed focuses on individual frames, or stills, of an ever-changing roster of movies. Some show a fair amount of action at the start, while others will take careful analysis and decent trivia knowledge to crack. With each wrong guess, a new still is revealed, hopefully adding enough extra information and context for you to guess the correct movie title.

With only six guesses at your disposal, you may need a little help guessing today’s Framed answer. To give you a hint, we’ve included some clues that will tease the title of the movie picked as today’s puzzle. If you’ve already failed today’s puzzle, or would just like to know the answer, we’ve detailed that as well.

How to play Framed

To play Framed you just need to follow these steps, in your browser of choice. Note that any Framed versions you find elsewhere on app stores or other storefronts are likely to be fakes.

  • Go into your browser and visit framed.wtf
  • Take a look at the still for today
  • Make a guess, if it’s correct, you will see the rewards screen
  • If incorrect, you have five more chances, each showing a new still.
