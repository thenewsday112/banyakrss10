How to stream Markiplier’s Iron Lung

📅 May 31, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Mark “Markiplier” Fischbach’s cinematic adaptation of Iron Lung was a monstrous success. The film’s lucrative, industry-shaping theatrical run ended in late February, and fans have been waiting with bated breath for the film to make its way to streaming.

During a panel at the 2026 Cannes Film Festival, Mark revealed Iron Lung would come to YouTube Movies and TV on May 31. However, like an unseen predator lurking in the lightless abyss, Iron Lung gave horror fans a heart-stopping shock when it suddenly splashed out across YouTube on May 29.

Iron Lung is out early on YouTube

In a video posted to his YouTube channel, Fischbach confirmed Iron Lung‘s sudden release wasn’t a mistake or a prank. Barely keeping his frustration with the site’s antiquated distribution model in check, he revealed Iron Lung is currently available for purchase or rental on YouTube in the United States, Australia, Canada, Great Britain, and New Zealand. He confirmed the film, with compatible subtitles, will make its way to other areas of the world soon.

Unfortunately for those who prefer more traditional streaming services, Iron Lung is a YouTube exclusive. In a livestream discussing the production of Iron Lung, Fischbach revealed he has no plans to release the film on any other streaming service. To ensure that he maintains control over the film’s distribution, Mark signed an exclusivity agreement with YouTube, meaning Iron Lung will probably never make its way to Netflix or HBO.

Iron Lung is currently available on YouTube for $4.99 to rent and $9.99 to purchase. Based on the video, Fischbach himself set the price, so it’s unlikely to change soon.

Will Iron Lung get a physical release?

Image via YouTube

Fischbach, in the same livestream where he outlined Iron Lung‘s streaming situation, also confirmed the film’s upcoming DVD and Blu-ray release. He revealed he bought a disc printer and is producing the physical releases himself.

In the video above, he also previewed the Blu-ray’s menu, which is a near-complete recreation of the Iron Lung submarine’s retro-computer interface. Complete with CRT lines and blocky text cursors, the monotone display flawlessly captures Iron Lung‘s grungy, ramshackle vibe. It seems this interface will be exclusive to the Blu-ray edition, but DVD buyers will get a full recreation of the Iron Lung game’s iconic menu, so there’s something for everyone.

Trump Takes Over as Freedom 250’s Great American State Fair Headliner After Several Artists Drop Out

📅 May 30, 2026  |  Category: Politics  |  Read Source →

An upcoming celebration of America’s 250th anniversary, Freedom 250 Presents: The Great American State Fair, recently had several musical guests back out because of the event’s connections to President Donald Trump. Now, Trump himself is slated to headline the festivities, event organizers said Saturday (May 30).

“I understand Artists are getting ‘the yips’ having to do with their performance,” Trump posted to his social media platform Truth Social on Saturday, adding that he was thinking of bringing “the man who some say is the Greatest President in History (THE GOAT!), DONALD J. TRUMP, to take the place of these highly paid, Third Rate ‘Artists.’”

The group organizing the June fair on Washington’s National Mall, Freedom 250, confirmed the billing in a statement, writing, “We are excited to announce that President Trump will personally kick off this historic celebration.”

Trump’s social media post twice referenced the event as being “Wednesday,” though the fair doesn’t start until June 25. The White House did not immediately clarify the discrepancy.

Danielle Alvarez, a spokesperson for Freedom 250, emphasized the broader fair that will run through July 10 includes an array of exhibits, family-friendly attractions, musical performances, flyovers and more.

Freedom 250 is billed as nonpartisan, but was launched last year by Trump and is led by a former State Department appointee from Trump’s first term. Several artists, including Bret Michaels, the Commodores and Martina McBride dropped out last week.

Michaels and other artists have said that they were misled about the theme of the shows, or were otherwise wary of being caught up in a political fight.

Other artists plan to attend, including Flo Rida, Fab Morvan of Milli Vanilli and Vanilla Ice. The latter’s representative previously said that the “Ice Ice Baby” rapper was “proud to help celebrate America’s 250th Anniversary!”

New year, new look. That’s right: Destructoid’s about to get a bit of a glow-up.

Don’t worry, though: the hot takes, deep dives, and undeniable sass you know and love aren’t going anywhere. We’d never trade personality for polish. What we are doing, though, is rebuilding the site with you—our loyal readers—in mind, because reading Destructoid articles should feel just as good as seeing a remaster announcement for your favorite childhood game.

Our goal with this redesign was simple: make Destructoid prettier, easier, and more fun to navigate, all while giving you guys new ways to interact with the content you care about. Ads may keep the lights on, but we want the reading experience to be tailored for you.

And the good news is you won’t have to wait long, since the redesign launches today, Jan. 27. Without further ado, let’s break down what you can expect.

Like what you like (literally!)

With the new design, you’ll be able to like articles on Destructoid. No comment required, no need to come up with a witty social media caption—just a quick way to show some appreciation for an article you enjoyed. Think of it as a silent stamp of approval, or a high-five to the author who spent an ungodly amount of time writing a guide about how to romance your favorite character.

These likes help us better understand what resonates with our readers, so you can see more of the content you actually want to read.

Save articles for later

Ever start reading an article only to get distracted and lose it in your endless void of browser tabs? Same.

With our new Saved Articles feature, you can now put a digital pin in them. Whether it’s a feature you plan to argue about with normies online, or a guide you want to keep open during your playthrough, you’ll have an easier way of finding that article a second time by saving it, opening your account page, and clicking on the link. Sounds easy, right? Yeah, that’s because it is.

Get ready for game hubs

This might just be the team’s personal favorite—game hubs. These bad boys are here to organize everything you need to know about a game in one place: its release date, story overview, number of play hours, and more. It’s also where you can rate the game with your own score, discuss it with other community members, and see all the cool stuff we publish about it on a single screen.

Seamless navigation and an easier-to-read layout

The biggest change of them all is by far the most noticeable. Our website is about to look a whole lot snazzier with clearer layouts, easier on-page navigation, and a new color scheme that simply screams Destructoid with crisp reds and brighter greens. It’ll be like Christmas all over again, except instead of Santa bringing presents, it’s our IT guy bringing a new web layout.

Whether you’re on mobile, your home setup, or checking in from work (don’t worry, we won’t snitch), the site should feel smoother and less cluttered. It’s a Christmas miracle, in January!

That’s not all, folks

Remember how we said this redesign was made with you in mind? Yeah, we intend to keep it that way. We’ll be iterating, tweaking, and improving as we go, but we won’t be able to do it without your help.

When the new design launches tomorrow, like some articles and save a few favorites. Maybe let us know how you truly feel about the latest Call of Duty by giving it your own score. If you run into bugs, glitches, or anything that just feels off, reach out to us at tips@destructoid.com.

Same Destructoid. New coat of paint. Let’s make this year a good one.
