How to get the Tickets to your Skate Buddy in Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3+4

📅 Jul 08, 2025  |  Category: Travel  |  Read Source →

Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3+4 brings you and your crew to a slew of different locations, so there’s bound to be a little travel involved. In the Airport level, you’re tasked with bringing tickets to your skate buddy so they don’t miss the plane. Causing mayhem is optional, but recommended.

Much like helping the Thin Man in Suburbia, this is a short two-part objective: pick up the tickets and bring them to your skate buddy. There’s a small catch here, though: you cannot fall down or go through the metal detectors, or else you’ll have to start the process again. Here’s how you can grab the tickets and bring them to your friend.

How to get the Tickets in Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3+4’s Airport level

It’d be much easier if you could just text them their boarding passes. Screenshot by Destructoid

The tickets are at an airline counter just to the left of the map’s starting point. Look behind the counter for the woman with her hand raised and grind in front of her to grab the tickets. Once you do, you cannot fail to land a combo, so play it safe if you’re not that confident in your skills.

Jump past the first set of escalators and keep going until you see the metal detectors. Use the conveyor belts as a kicker to boost yourself above the sensors without triggering them. Activating the beep or bailing here means you must go back to the start. After that, go down another set of escalators and you’ll be in the area with the boarding gates, where your skate buddy is located.

Where to find your skate buddy in Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3+4’s Airport level

Use the flags as a guide if you’re lost. Screenshot by Destructoid

Once you’ve made it to the main terminal, make a left and look for your skate buddy at gate C2, where the next flight to Tokyo is boarding. Two of your friends are by the second gate on the left, just across from the flags of Great Britain and Germany. One guy is wearing a red hoodie and has a skateboard at his feet, accompanied by a woman in a pink jacket and ripped jeans. Bring them the tickets and wrap up this objective. As you’re roaming around the airport, the PA system will shoot out some cheeky voice lines—including a reference to comedy classic Airplane!

If you still need to cross a few more objectives off your list, go through the X-ray machine on the counter on the right of the spawn area, across from where you got your tickets. This leads you to another part of the map, where you can Airwalk over an escalator and grab a couple of secrets.

How to Help the Thin Man in Suburbia – Tony Hawk Pro Skater 3+4

📅 Jul 08, 2025  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

There’s something afoot in Suburbia in Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3+4. Helping the Thin Man gives you an idea of the sinister forces at play—but it also opens up the way to the Secret Tape and a couple of really cool ramps, so naturally, that’s where we’re headed.

Time for some remodeling. Screenshot by Destructoid

Helping the Thin Man is a two-part objective in the Suburbia map. He’s located in the totally-not-haunted house behind the spawn in Suburbia, which you can find by grinding or jumping on a railing near the decorated ramps, or just by turning around when dropping.

Standing near this mysterious stranger ends up with him asking for an axe (subtle!), and he sucker punches you if you get too close. But if you’re looking for some goals to complete before unlocking the next arena, here’s how you can help him in THPS 3+4—even if he is kind of a jerk.

Helping the Thin Man in Suburbia in Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3+4

You have my skate. And his axe. Screenshot by Destructoid

To help the Thin Man, you first need an axe. Find it by the construction site to the front-right of the spawn, where the letter E in S-K-A-T-E is. Grind a short rail close to the ground, pick up the axe, and return to the Thin Man.

Once you’ve handed him the axe, the Thin Man will do his best Jack Torrance impression and tear down the wall leading inside the house. From there, you can trick into a new area. The fact that the first gap you encounter is called I Got a Bad Feeling About This is probably nothing to be alarmed about.

Screenshot by Destructoid

The new area is a good spot for doing tricks, but more importantly, it leads straight into the Secret Tape for Suburbia. Break the window onto the top floor of the house, then go straight ahead and grind the American flag to get the Secret Tape and knock out some progress toward your next goal. Is it still an abandoned house if there’s someone locked in as a prisoner?

How to Nosegrind around the Horn in Canada – Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3+4

📅 Jul 08, 2025  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

If you’re a veteran of the powerhouse franchise, Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3+4 offers plenty of nostalgia, down to the gap-based objectives like performing a Nosegrind around the Horn in the game’s second map, Canada.

While it can seem deceptively tricky, learning how to Nosegrind around the Horn is fairly simple. The first step is finding the spot in question, and the second is hitting your trick right so the game counts it as a Nosegrind. Here’s how you can complete that objective.

That’s the grind you need to hit. Screenshot by Destructoid

How to find the Horn in Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3+4

The Horn is the tall tree with a hole in the middle on the frozen side of the map. It’s by the circular halfpipe encircling another, but the quickest way to get there is to turn around from where you spawn.

Do a 180 turn and look for the ledges. Screenshot by Destructoid

Drop from the first ramp, then get some distance and turn around. Speed toward the ramp and jump to a wooden ledge just across from you, then hit the brakes as soon as you touch the ground. Ollie, No Comply, and Boneless (used by pressing forward once/twice before jumping) can give you a little bit of height if you’re falling short.

After jumping on the first ledge, hop to another wooden path in front of you and either grind the rail or hold the down button to break your fall. Go through the hole in the tree, then turn around and find the small rail to the left side. You must grind this rail for a few seconds and land a Nosegrind with the Around The Horn gap to complete this objective. Hit the gap, land the combo, and cross this one off your list.

How to Nosegrind the Horn in Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3+4

Don’t botch the landing and you’re in the clear. Screenshot by Destructoid

To make sure you’re hitting the Nosegrind, jump onto the rail using the front of your board. Usually, a simple forward + grind command works, but if you’re still landing a different trick, try to hit your grind head-on.

You need to hold the Nosegrind for around three to five seconds, which isn’t a lot of time. The Around The Horn gap will pop before you hit the ledge with the traffic cones, so it’s easy to bag two objectives for the price of one and keep on unlocking the new tracks. Rio is waiting, after all.
