The Los Angeles shoegaze band released the track as the final preview of their fifth studio album ‘ZIRP!’, which was released today (October 30) via Fantasy Recordings. Order your copy here.

‘Dare Me’ follows the previous singles ‘Trillions’, ‘Kid’ and ‘The Fountain’, and sees the band channelling their trademark hazy guitar tones into what frontman Zachary Cole Smith has described as a song about the hope of building a new world amid societal collapse.

F.B.I. director Kash Patel popped the question to his country singer girlfriend, Alexis Wilkins, over the weekend. In an Instagram post on Sunday (Oct. 4), Wilkins proudly announced the news in a post in which the couple both smiled as Wilkins held up her engagement ring. “Went and got the law involved 💍❤️,” she cheekily captioned the pic.

According to The New York Times, Patel, 46, and Wilkins, 27, met at a party in fall 2022 and at country singer John Rich’s Nashville home, where a publicist had brought along Wilkins. The paper reported that Patel complimented Wilkins’ jacket that night and by early 2023 the couple were dating. Wilkins was also in the gallery during Patel’s combative Senate confirmation hearing, with Wilkins saying she was attracted to Patel’s honesty and his “incredible” values and character during an interview on The Megyn Kelly Show last July.

Patel’s relationship with Wilkins — who signed with WME and artist management company the AMG in 2020 — has come under scrutiny over the past year over claims that the nation’s top law enforcement agent had used taxpayer funds to pay for his trips to see Wilkins perform, as well as for the couple’s date nights. The Times also reported that the F.B.I. had dispatched tactical agents to drive the singer to hair appointments and to stand guard at a senior center in Dixon, Ill. where she sang for a small group of young conservatives.

Though SWAT teams are typically trained to arrest violent criminals, free hostages and break up terror cells, former F.B.I. agents told the paper that Patel’s reported demand that a rotating group of SWAT officers provide security for Wilkins during her gigs, personal appearances and errands is unprecedented. An F.B.I. spokesperson told the Times that the full-time security detail for the civilian romantic partner of a federal official was necessary due to a reported “hundreds” of death threats aimed at the singer; the paper noted that the spouses of cabinet and congressional officials do not routinely receive full-time government-funded security details.

To date, Wilkins, whose LinkedIn says she’s been a recording artist, performer and songwriter since August 2007 (when she would have been eight years old), has not released a full-length album, but has issued the six-song 2023 EP Grit, featuring the songs “Quite Like Whiskey,” “Heaven In a Stetson” and “Half as Cowboy,” and a handful of singles.

Trump Takes Over as Freedom 250’s Great American State Fair Headliner After Several Artists Drop Out

📅 May 30, 2026  |  Category: Politics  |  Read Source →

An upcoming celebration of America’s 250th anniversary, Freedom 250 Presents: The Great American State Fair, recently had several musical guests back out because of the event’s connections to President Donald Trump. Now, Trump himself is slated to headline the festivities, event organizers said Saturday (May 30).

“I understand Artists are getting ‘the yips’ having to do with their performance,” Trump posted to his social media platform Truth Social on Saturday, adding that he was thinking of bringing “the man who some say is the Greatest President in History (THE GOAT!), DONALD J. TRUMP, to take the place of these highly paid, Third Rate ‘Artists.’”

The group organizing the June fair on Washington’s National Mall, Freedom 250, confirmed the billing in a statement, writing, “We are excited to announce that President Trump will personally kick off this historic celebration.”

Trump’s social media post twice referenced the event as being “Wednesday,” though the fair doesn’t start until June 25. The White House did not immediately clarify the discrepancy.

Danielle Alvarez, a spokesperson for Freedom 250, emphasized the broader fair that will run through July 10 includes an array of exhibits, family-friendly attractions, musical performances, flyovers and more.

Freedom 250 is billed as nonpartisan, but was launched last year by Trump and is led by a former State Department appointee from Trump’s first term. Several artists, including Bret Michaels, the Commodores and Martina McBride dropped out last week.

Michaels and other artists have said that they were misled about the theme of the shows, or were otherwise wary of being caught up in a political fight.

Other artists plan to attend, including Flo Rida, Fab Morvan of Milli Vanilli and Vanilla Ice. The latter’s representative previously said that the “Ice Ice Baby” rapper was “proud to help celebrate America’s 250th Anniversary!”
