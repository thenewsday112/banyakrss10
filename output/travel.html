America's Clocks Are Still Falling Back Tonight Despite Trump's Daylight Saving Push

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Travel  |  Read Source →
Clocks "fall back" one hour tonight to end Daylight Saving Time — though many Americans want to end the twice-yearly clock change, reports Axios. But the U.S. Congress "remains deadlocked about how to make that happen." The clocks are falling back despite a bipartisan House vote in July to make daylight saving time permanent and Trump's subsequent push for Senate action... While most Americans want to end the twice-yearly clock changes, they don't necessarily favor Trump's solution. Gallup found 48% of Americans support permanent standard time the entire year, compared with 24% who prefer permanent daylight saving time, as Trump wants... In 1974, the U.S. experimented with year-round daylight saving time but reversed course amid concerns about children going to school in the dark>. Experts still debate whether permanent DST is good for people. Supporters argue that permanent daylight saving time would provide more afternoon and evening sunlight, encouraging outdoor activities. But sleep experts warn that darker mornings and lighter evenings can disrupt sleep cycles, while permanent daylight saving time could force millions of children to travel to school in the dark. The bottom line: The American Academy of Sleep Medicine says it prefers permanent standard time because it better aligns with natural sleep cycles. "Hawaii and most of Arizona don't observe daylight saving time, so their clocks won't change," the article points out, adding that eliminating it for the rest of America "still faces opposition in the Senate and isn't expected to be considered by the end of the year. But "the upcoming midterms could change the balance of power in Congress, potentially shifting the debate."

How to get the Tickets to your Skate Buddy in Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3+4

📅 Jul 08, 2025  |  Category: Travel  |  Read Source →

Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3+4 brings you and your crew to a slew of different locations, so there’s bound to be a little travel involved. In the Airport level, you’re tasked with bringing tickets to your skate buddy so they don’t miss the plane. Causing mayhem is optional, but recommended.

Much like helping the Thin Man in Suburbia, this is a short two-part objective: pick up the tickets and bring them to your skate buddy. There’s a small catch here, though: you cannot fall down or go through the metal detectors, or else you’ll have to start the process again. Here’s how you can grab the tickets and bring them to your friend.

How to get the Tickets in Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3+4’s Airport level

It’d be much easier if you could just text them their boarding passes. Screenshot by Destructoid

The tickets are at an airline counter just to the left of the map’s starting point. Look behind the counter for the woman with her hand raised and grind in front of her to grab the tickets. Once you do, you cannot fail to land a combo, so play it safe if you’re not that confident in your skills.

Jump past the first set of escalators and keep going until you see the metal detectors. Use the conveyor belts as a kicker to boost yourself above the sensors without triggering them. Activating the beep or bailing here means you must go back to the start. After that, go down another set of escalators and you’ll be in the area with the boarding gates, where your skate buddy is located.

Where to find your skate buddy in Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 3+4’s Airport level

Use the flags as a guide if you’re lost. Screenshot by Destructoid

Once you’ve made it to the main terminal, make a left and look for your skate buddy at gate C2, where the next flight to Tokyo is boarding. Two of your friends are by the second gate on the left, just across from the flags of Great Britain and Germany. One guy is wearing a red hoodie and has a skateboard at his feet, accompanied by a woman in a pink jacket and ripped jeans. Bring them the tickets and wrap up this objective. As you’re roaming around the airport, the PA system will shoot out some cheeky voice lines—including a reference to comedy classic Airplane!

If you still need to cross a few more objectives off your list, go through the X-ray machine on the counter on the right of the spawn area, across from where you got your tickets. This leads you to another part of the map, where you can Airwalk over an escalator and grab a couple of secrets.

With NEXT update, No Man’s Sky finally brings it all together

📅 Aug 06, 2018  |  Category: Travel  |  Read Source →

Nearly two years after its rocky initial launch, No Man’s Sky still has a long way to go. But after three major updates and the recent pseudo-relaunch in the form of No Man’s Sky NEXT, it finally feels like a game that deserves to go that extra distance. That’s assuming you can put up with a lot of turbulent interstellar travel in the meantime, anyway.

Like a lot of folks, I whipped up a fresh save to coincide with the new NEXT update after barely touching the game’s reportedly great additions (base building, freighters, and a whole new story campaign) over the past two years. The restart immediately felt both familiar and bizarrely off, somehow. My silent avatar still started on one of 18 quintillion procedurally generated planets with nothing but a mining laser and a damaged ship.

But there was a new, subdued urgency to those opening seconds. The game casually drew my attention to a half-full energy meter on my user interface. I only had seconds to find sodium and refuel it, or I’d start taking damage from the superheated planet’s surface. Finding sodium, however, meant I needed to repair my multi-tool’s malfunctioning scanner. That required a material that was new to me, Ferrite Dust, and much futzing with reams of menus and inventories.
